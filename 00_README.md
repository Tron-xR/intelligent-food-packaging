## One-line Summary

A decision-support system that ingests a food commodity's physical/chemical properties and storage/transport conditions, and outputs a ranked packaging material + specification recommendation (OTR, WVTR, thickness, MAP gas mix, etc.), backed by a rules+ML hybrid recommendation engine and a curated packaging material knowledge base.
